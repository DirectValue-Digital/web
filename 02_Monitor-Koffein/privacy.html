Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Protecting your personal data is a key concern for us. The app "Monitor-Koffein" (hereinafter "App") has been developed in accordance with the principles of data minimization and avoidance.

1. Controller

Responsible for data processing is:
See Legal Notice (Impressum)

2. Collection and Processing of Personal Data

The App does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or telemetry data.

3. Network Connections and Data Transmission

The App is a pure offline application. It does not establish any internet or network connections and does not communicate with external servers. No data is transmitted to the developer or third parties.

4. Access to Device Resources and Permissions

The App runs within the secured macOS App Sandbox. It does not request permissions for sensitive system components (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos). The App only accesses the following macOS system APIs:

  • IOKit (Power Management): To temporarily prevent the display from sleeping while the App is activated by the user.
  • ServiceManagement: To optionally register the App as a startup item (autostart at login), provided the user explicitly enables this in the App's settings.

5. Local Storage

No personal data or profiles are stored locally on your device. Only the settings chosen by the user (e.g., whether the App should load automatically at system startup) are saved via macOS system services.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not process any data from you, there is no data we could output, correct, or delete. For questions about the App or security, you can contact us at support@directvalue.de.

3. Netzwerkverbindungen und Datenübermittlung

Die App ist eine reine Offline-Anwendung. Sie baut keine Internet- oder Netzwerkverbindungen auf und kommuniziert nicht mit externen Servern. Es findet keine Übermittlung von Daten an den Entwickler oder Dritte statt.
